Privacy

Sighting is a lost and found board for pets. It collects as little as it can. Last updated November 2026.

What leaves your device

  • Your account. Your email and password sign you in. They are kept on our own server on Cloudflare, with the password hashed. You can delete your account from inside the app or the site at any time.
  • Your posts. Lost and found posts, their photos and the area you post in are public, so neighbours can help.
  • Email. Sign-up and password reset emails are sent through Resend.
  • Site visits. The website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you across sites. The apps have no analytics.

What we never do

No ads. No selling data. No tracking.

Kids

Sighting is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.
