# Sighting Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 2026*

Sighting is a lost pet network. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Your account.** Your email and hashed password are kept on our own server on Cloudflare. You can delete your account from inside the app or the site.
- **Posts.** Lost and found posts, photos and the area you post in are public so neighbours can help.
- **Email.** Sign-up and password reset emails are sent through Resend.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites. The apps have no analytics.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Sighting isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
